A polished, point-and-click Windows cleanup, debloat, and optimization app. Quick Clean for a fast tidy-up, Full Clean when you want everything, plus live system health, performance tracking, and safe gaming tweaks — all in one dark, no-nonsense app.

Watchblox

Latest: v1.4.1 · Windows 10/11 (64-bit)

Know when your friends play. A Roblox friend & presence companion for Windows. Enter your username once and get a live dashboard of who's online, in-game, or in Studio — with a Join button that drops you straight into a friend's exact server. No browser, no extension, no login needed.

  • Live presence dashboard with avatars & game names
  • One-click Join into a friend's exact server
  • Activity feed & Windows notifications
  • Profiles, mutual friends & friend network graph
  • Public data only — no cookie, no telemetry
