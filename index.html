What each app actually does

Sarah's Toolkit

Clean, debloat, optimize

Cleanup

Quick Clean is the fast one, temps, caches, shaders, browser junk, done in seconds and it tells you how much it freed. Full Clean goes further, update caches, log bloat, all of it, and you can watch it go.

Debloat

only junk preinstalled apps show up, and only ones actually installed on your pc. nothing gets removed unless you check it. edge, copilot, xbox / game bar, onedrive, solitaire, clipchamp, media player, films & tv, and office hub never show up at all.

Optimize & Customize

registry tweaks for privacy, gaming, and performance. the app shows whats actually applied, and every tweak can be reverted in one click. same thing for taskbar, explorer, and start menu tweaks.

Health & Tools

a 0-100 health score from disk space, updates, drive health, and battery health. plus boot time, per disk ssd health, and a performance tracker that logs cpu, ram, and temps while you game. tools has ssd retrim, dns selector, service optimizer, startup manager, network rescue, game ping test, and defender exclusions for apps i know are safe.

Installs fast

self contained, no .net download, no prerequisites, installs in seconds. it updates itself and your settings stay.

Watchblox

See when your friends are playing

Live dashboard

put your roblox username in once and it keeps a live board of your friends, whos offline, online, in game, or in studio. avatars, game names, search, filters, all there.

Join a friend's exact server

when a friend is in game, one join button drops you straight into their exact server. no browser, no extension, you just need roblox player installed.

Activity feed & notifications

every coming and going gets logged in an activity feed, and windows notifications tell you the second a friend hops into a game. so you dont have to keep checking.

Profiles, mutuals & the friend graph

open any friends profile for their avatar, bio, join date, groups, and roles. mutuals shows who you have in common, and the network graph maps your whole friend circle. drag to pan, scroll to zoom.

Private by design

no login or cookie needed, everything comes from robloxs public data. no telemetry, no accounts, and all your settings and history stay on your pc in %LOCALAPPDATA%\Watchblox. you can watch anyone by username too, even if theyre not your friend.

Are both apps really free?

yeah. no cost, no ads, no paywall, no accounts. download it, install it, use it.

Why does Windows SmartScreen warn me?

both installers are unsigned, signing certs cost money and these are free hobby projects. smartscreen flags any unsigned installer. click more info then run anyway. both projects are public on github if you want to check them.

Do I need to install .NET or anything else first?

no. sarahs toolkit is self contained, it brings its own runtime and installs in seconds. watchblox only needs roblox player if you want to use join, the dashboard, feed, and notifications work without it.

Does Watchblox need my Roblox password or login?

no. friend lists and presence are public roblox data, so no cookie, password, or login needed. nothing about your account gets sent anywhere, no telemetry at all.

Is Sarah's Toolkit safe? Will it delete something I need?

cleanup only touches temps, caches, and junk. debloat only removes apps you check, and it never lists apps people actually use, like edge, copilot, xbox, onedrive, and the rest. tweaks show their live state and can be reverted in one click.

What versions of Windows do they work on?

windows 10 and 11, 64-bit, for both apps.

How do updates work?

both apps update themselves, install once and youre good. the download buttons here always point to the latest release too.

Where do I report a bug or request a feature?

open an issue on the projects github page, sarahs toolkit or watchblox. thats the fastest way to reach me.
